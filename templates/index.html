About FoodShare

Small Acts of Sharing Can Make a Big Difference.

FoodShare is a food donation platform that connects restaurants, hotels, event organizers and households with NGOs.

Our mission is to reduce food waste and ensure that surplus food reaches people who need it.

✓ Easy food donation process
✓ Direct connection with NGOs

How FoodShare Works

Three simple steps to make a difference in someone's life.

🍱

01. Donate Food

Donors add surplus food details, quantity, pickup location and suitable pickup time.

🤝

02. NGO Requests

NGOs browse available food donations and request the food they need.

❤️

03. Help People

Approved food donations reach people who need support.
